Our amazing logo designer, Michooski, revamped our logo after more consideration, and we polled our Discord community to see which they preferred. We’re going with the new iteration for most uses of a logo, as it fits better in square dimensions and is more recognizable. But there will still be cases where we can use the full-body penguin, as either a kind of mascot, future merch, or maybe letterheads, etc.
