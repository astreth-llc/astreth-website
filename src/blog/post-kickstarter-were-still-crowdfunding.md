#### Our Kickstarter Campaign is Over

So yeah, that happened! We launched a Kickstarter campaign from mid-December to mid-January, 4 weeks long. (Kickstarter said campaigns that are shorter than 30 days do better.) Though our pledgers gave generously, we likely didn’t reach enough of our audience to secure the goal we set. But fear not, Pebblers! Our mission continues.

#### Crowdfunding Continues

Though our Kickstarter campaign did not reach its goal, we are still accepting [donations](/donate) directly on our webpage. You can expect that page to start expanding and getting a more informative overhaul, too!

@Tea, one of the members in our Discord, suggested we post micro-goals so people can see exactly what the next step in our funding goal is. We had already planned to post a transparency breakdown on our projected costs for 2026, so this fits right in line with what our community is thinking, too.

Be on the lookout here for a more expansive Donation page coming soon, so you can feel like you’re behind the scenes on where the project is at, where it’s going, and how we’re going to get there.

*Update: our [Pebbler campaign page](/pebbler) is now live, with the full plan and backer tiers.*

Thank you for everyone’s belief in this mission, in us, and for how you’re letting your voice be heard so that this can represent you—our wonderful community.

Slow and steady wins the race, Pebblers!
