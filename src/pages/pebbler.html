Our Origin Story

It all started with a viral TikTok from Tallgirl6234 during the 2024 TikTok ban. She called on Tech TikTok to rally together and build a platform by us, for us — and in just one week, over 4,500 people joined her Discord to help make it real.

Since then, our community has spent over a year refining the mission, values, and foundation needed to take on a challenge this big, with long-term thinking and care.

Our app name, Pebbler, is inspired by the penguin ritual of gifting pebbles: a symbol of care, connection, and belonging. When our community learned about “pebbling” (the act of sharing moments to show you’re thinking of someone), the name won a community poll with 93% support. It already felt like home!

Now we have a complete proof-of-concept design and a dedicated team of volunteer developers ready to start building. Every feature has been shaped with community feedback and built to grow stronger over time.

Our Kickstarter campaign ended in January 2026, and we’re still crowdfunding right here.

Open-Source & Decentralized

We had an entire early version of Pebbler built in a traditional, centralized framework. Then we made the hard choice to stop, reassess, and rebuild from scratch, because our mission is bigger than launching fast.

Our pivot to decentralized

When we discovered an emerging decentralized ecosystem (called AT Protocol) where users truly own their data and identity, we knew we had to make the shift, even if it meant learning a whole new architecture and adding another year to our timeline.

Bluesky is the company driving this network forward, and it already has millions of daily active users. That means Pebbler can launch into an active ecosystem, greatly reducing the risk of an empty or isolated platform.

Open source for collaboration

One of our values is collaboration. We believe open-sourcing our project allows for more community creativity and innovation that benefits all of society. Open-sourcing will happen gradually and responsibly as the project matures.

A decentralized foundation means:

  • You control your data
  • You control your identity
  • You can take your account with you
  • No one platform can trap you
  • No company (including us) owns your social life

This wasn’t the easy path. It was the right one.

Our Commitments

Before writing a single line of code, we spent months designing the organization behind the app, because if that foundation isn’t right, nothing else will be. Read more about our values.

  1. Community-Owned Ethos

    We build for the people who use Pebbler, not distant investors. Growth is not the goal. Goodness is.

  2. Ethical Design

    No dark patterns. No manipulation. No infinite-scroll traps. No algorithmic FOMO. Just connection, and intentional, joyful, human features.

  3. Your Data = Your Power

    We won’t sell user data, mine your attention, or engineer addiction. Your data is your own, across other apps using AT Protocol or even on your own servers.

  4. A Platform That Gives Back

    We aim to become a Benefit Corporation and remain privately held, share a significant portion of profits with the community, and keep creator fees as low as possible.

  5. Built to Heal What Social Media Broke

    We’re designing away from performative pressure, harmful comparison, burnout, divisive algorithms, doom-scrolling, and rage-bait. We want to leave your nervous system better than we found it.

Big-Picture Features

We have dozens of ideas for the future, but here are a few we’re already designing. All of them are subject to change with more community feedback.

  • Reputation

    Bringing accountability into social media

    Pebbler rewards good character, respect, and community trust instead of outrage. You earn reputation through patterns of positive participation, with human moderators verifying changes. It’s recoverable, never public shaming.

  • Community Moderation

    Empowering the people who care

    Moderation power goes to those who’ve earned trust: community-trusted moderators, reports that carry more weight from high-reputation members, and safer spaces without corporate algorithms deciding what’s allowed.

  • Featured Badges

    Identity, credibility, and fun

    Badges help members express themselves and show their positive contributions. Earn them by contributing meaningfully, gaining community trust, reaching milestones, or joining special experiences.

  • Customizable Streams

    Total control of your feed

    Create multiple feeds for your mood: cute animals only, current events and science, creative inspiration, or no politics at all. You control every filter, see why recommendations appear, and can turn off AI-generated content entirely.

  • Expert Comments

    Giving credible voices a better platform

    An optional way to view comments from verified professionals in a post’s field, such as doctors, scientists, and journalists. Think peer-reviewing, but not just for scientific articles.

Future App Releases

These features roll out in stages and are influenced by further funding, testing, and community input.

  1. Pebbler 2.0 Refinement & Trust Foundations

    • Ongoing bug fixes and performance improvements
    • Private messaging
    • Expert Comments, starting with medical and science fields

  2. Pebbler 3.0 Transparency & Community Voice

    • Continued stabilization and refinements
    • Journalist credential verification
    • “Trusted News Source” badge
    • Member feature voting to guide development priorities

  3. Pebbler 4.0 Communities & Empowerment

    • Community spaces
    • Community-appointed representatives and moderators
    • Tools that give communities more control over their own spaces

  4. Pebbler 5.0 Sustainability & Giving Back

    • Creative, ethical monetization
    • Features that allow us to give back to communities
    • Community-voted initiatives and experiments
