OUR STORY

There are a lot of problems in the world, especially now. TikToker and social media influencer, Tallgirl6234, proposed, "We have a lot of talented people in this community–why don't we get together and make our own solutions?"

She created a Discord community in response to the overwhelming interest, and within its first week, membership blew up from 100 users to over 2,600. Busy with other projects, Tallgirl handed this off to those who had app-building/business experience, and let us run with the mission. We spent a few weeks compiling everyone's ideas, and researched the best company format for co-operative management.

With necessary legal protections in place, a clear brand and leadership structure, we're now ready to turn on the green light for fundraising. We need everyone's help to afford the costs of running a business, hiring talent, and officially starting the app-building process. Now we're really getting to the good stuff!

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to create a company built on shared values and good business ethics. To operate with transparency and prioritize our community's needs over shareholders or the super-rich.

And of course, to develop an app by our community, for our community–a space to entertain, educate, inform, and connect.

OUR VALUES

  • INTEGRITY

    Our actions are in line with our beliefs. Our business principles and practices are in line with our values and mission. We live them out with transparency and are committed to correcting any type of hypocrisy when it is noticed or called out.

  • EMPATHY

    We strive to understand, share, and respond to others' feelings and perspectives. We seek to understand before seeking to be understood.

  • HARMONY

    We believe collaboration and cooperation lead to the best ideas or outcomes. We value teamwork, listening to different opinions with openness, and approaching new ideas with a "Yes, and..." philosophy.

  • EQUITY

    We consider all people valuable, and give opportunities not based on bias or privilege or equality, but according to need and ability level. This is to support our belief of harmony, empathy, and diversity.

  • AUTONOMY

    We respect individual work-styles and trust our workers as motivated members of the company, which allows for greater flexibility, innovation, and efficiency.

  • DEMOCRACY

    We put the power of company decisions and direction in the hands of its members-the people. Any representatives of the company are elected by its members and can be unelected if necessary.

  • RESPECT

    We regard the feelings, wishes, rights, or traditions of others, even when they are different than our own.

  • DIVERSITY

    We include people from a range of different social and ethnic backgrounds, genders, sexual orientations, and neurotypes, etc.
