About Astreth

OUR STORY

There are a lot of problems in the world, especially now. Social Media influencer, Tallgirl6234, proposed, "We have a lot of talented people in this community–why don't we get together and make our own solutions?" and now here we are!

OUR MISSION

To create a company built on shared values and business ethics. To develop an app by our community, for our community–a space to entertain, educate, inform, and connect.

OUR VALUES

Astreth is built on values of integrity, democracy, equity, autonomy, harmony, empathy, respect, and diversity. We prioritize honesty and transparency, ensuring every voice is heard and treated fairly.
