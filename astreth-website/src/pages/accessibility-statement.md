Thank you for visiting Astreth.com. We are committed to making our website usable by all people, including those with disabilities. Our goal is to meet or exceed W3C’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, so that visiting our site is an amazing experience for everyone. However, achieving that goal is an ongoing process, and we ask for your patience and understanding as we work toward it.

While we use tools that enable our content creators to build features that are accessible to all, we also use third-party applications such as Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Instagram that may not hold the same commitment we do. Therefore, some individuals may face challenges when accessing those third-party applications that are beyond our ability to control or correct.

We also ask for your help to achieve our goal. If you have difficulty accessing content, viewing a file, or notice any other accessibility problems please contact us at [Help@Astreth.com](mailto:Help@Astreth.com?subject=Accessibility) and specify the nature of the accessibility issue and assistance technology you use. Thank you again for visiting Astreth.com
