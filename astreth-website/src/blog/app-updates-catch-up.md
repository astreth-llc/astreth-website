## **App Name Finalist?**

Hey **@everyone**, We analyzed the finalists from the first round of the app name poll, but sadly, most of them were already taken or their domain name was unavailable for purchase. We kinda need an app name soon, so we started the **⁠💡⛈ : App Name Brainstorm** again to see if we could get lucky and strike on something we all could love that wasn’t taken, and we came up with “OurVox”! It represents our mission, is free as a company name, and has several website domains available that aren’t expensive. 8 people voted and liked OurVox, and 4 liked the Arvox spelled version. Marketing prefers OurVox for clarity and branding. Since it got such a positive response from so many in such a short time, we thought it could be an instant winner! Still, we really wanted to give everyone a chance to weigh in on the name to make sure it reflects the community’s will. So please take a minute to cast your vote on our mission poll channel by Wed. 5pm PT if OurVox sounds like a good app name for our mission! **⁠mission-polls⁠** And thank you for your participation and support! We’re doing it! (edited)
