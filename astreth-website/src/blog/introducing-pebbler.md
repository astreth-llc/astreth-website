This month we sent out our first official newsletter update for all things going on with Astreth. We’d like to keep you updated on our mission progress at least once a month, and with our influx of new volunteers, that should be much easier. After all, “Many hands make light work!”

We’ve onboarded at least 70 new volunteers in the past few weeks, thanks to Tallgirl’s video that went mini-viral. Now that the holidays are over, we’re all back to the work and making great progress. (We hope you had Happy Holidays, btw!)

Let’s see, what else—so much has been happening!

## Winning App Name & Logo

If you’re in our Discord, you may have noticed the poll we just conducted to get everyone’s input on our app name and logo. Well, 93% of you were happy with what the design team came up with, so we’re excited to announce to you our winning app name and logo:

<figure>
<img src="/assets/img/pebbler-logo-original.webp" alt="The original Pebbler logo: a penguin above the word Pebbler" width="556" height="416" loading="lazy">
</figure>

The name Pebbler was inspired by a tweet our volunteer, Brave, saw on Christmas eve. And that’s exactly what we all are—a bunch of pebblers pebbling our way through these crazy times, huddling close to keep each other warmed and encouraged as we weather the storms of life, together. 🐧 🪨

Even though we almost had our MVP coded, we decided to pivot and rebuild it using ATProtocol. This will allow us to decentralize, which is more in line with our mission to put people over profit, and to keep the power in the hands of our Pebblers. 😊 It may take us a bit more time to publish the app, but we believe will be worth it in the long-run.

Thank you again to everyone helping us spread the word on social media. We'll be adding our social media links to our Astreth.com website as soon as we can. Anyone who wants to be an appbassador can interact with our accounts and share our posts. It really helps if we all do a little!

After all, in the words of Hellen Keller:

### “Alone we can do so little; together we can do ***so much***.”
