#### Volunteer Process Update

This is a reminder that our "Volunteer to Founder" process has been finalized. This structured yet flexible pathway is designed to support a smooth transition from volunteer roles to Founder positions within our group. It’s an initiative focused on fostering organic growth, enabling dedicated volunteers to gradually take on more responsibility and evolve into Founders.

Here is the process:

1. Submit your volunteer interest form so we know you’re ready to dig into the work with us!
2. Submit an application to express your interest in becoming a Founder. (If you did this in the old process, great! You won’t need to do this again.)
3. A Blob Rep will reach out and get you plugged into the blobs/teams you expressed interest in.
4. Once plugged in and official, coordinate with your Blob Rep to start working on projects.
5. After familiarizing ourselves with your work, a Founder may nominate you for Foundership. (If you've been working with us for several weeks and still haven’t heard anything, feel free to check in with your Blob Rep.)
6. Once you've been nominated, current Founders will discuss your application and volunteer efforts. At this point, if more information is needed, they may reach out to you for an interview.
7. After a week of discussion, Founders vote: If a 2/3 majority feel confident you meet all the requirements and would make a good addition, someone will reach out to you about becoming a Founder officially.

   Alternatively, if a 2/3 majority are not confident with adding you at that time, you have the option to continue volunteering. This will give Founders an opportunity to get to know you better as they work with you. The founder group may then hold another vote in the future, after gaining the additional experience.
8. Once the Founder Agreement is signed and the dues paid, we'll announce you to the Discord group as the newest Founder, and onboard you to the current processes, expectations, norms, etc.

We would be honored to work with you!
